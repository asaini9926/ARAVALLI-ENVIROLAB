02 · Material & Mineral Laboratory

Construction material & mineral testing.

Testing is organized by material category so engineers, contractors, and project owners can quickly identify the exact laboratory tests required for quality control, procurement, and billing certification.

Testing strictly adheres to Bureau of Indian Standards (IS), ASTM, and MoRTH specifications. On-site sample collection is available across Jaipur and Kotputli-Behror districts.
01 · SOIL & EARTHWORK

Soil & Earthwork Materials

  • Grain size distribution / Sieve analysis
  • Atterberg limits (Liquid & Plastic limit)
  • Moisture content & Field density
  • Specific gravity
  • Standard & Modified Proctor compaction
  • California Bearing Ratio (CBR - soaked/unsoaked)
02 · CEMENT

Cement

  • Fineness by Blaine / sieve
  • Standard consistency
  • Initial & final setting time
  • Soundness (Le-Chatelier & Autoclave)
  • Compressive strength (3, 7, 28 days)
  • Specific gravity
03 · CONCRETE

Concrete

  • Compressive strength (Cubes / Cylinders)
  • Flexural strength of beams
  • Split tensile strength
  • Density & Water absorption
  • Concrete mix design assistance
  • Non-Destructive Testing (Rebound hammer)
04 · AGGREGATES

Aggregates (Coarse & Fine)

  • Sieve analysis & Gradation curves
  • Specific gravity & Water absorption
  • Aggregate Crushing Value (ACV)
  • Aggregate Impact Value (AIV)
  • Los Angeles Abrasion Value (LAA)
  • Flakiness & Elongation indices
05 · MASONRY

Bricks, Blocks & Pavers

  • Compressive strength of clay & fly-ash bricks
  • Water absorption test
  • Dimensions & tolerance verification
  • Density and physical properties
  • Efflorescence testing
  • Paver block breaking load & abrasion
06 · STEEL

Steel & Reinforcement

  • Tensile strength & Proof stress
  • Yield strength (TMT bars)
  • Percentage elongation
  • Bend and rebend testing
  • Weight per meter / nominal mass
  • Chemical composition analysis
07 · BITUMEN

Bituminous Materials

  • Penetration test
  • Softening point (Ring & Ball)
  • Ductility test
  • Flash & Fire point
  • Specific gravity of bitumen
  • Bitumen viscosity & extraction testing
08 · MINERALS & ROCKS

Minerals & Rocks

  • Rock / mineral identification & petrography
  • Specific gravity, density & porosity
  • Unconfined Compressive Strength (UCS)
  • Loss on ignition (LOI)
  • Chemical & elemental assay
  • Limestone, marble, granite & sandstone
