      Regulatory Brief

      CTE or CTO? Know what your project needs.

      Under the Water Act (1974) and Air Act (1981), State Pollution Control Boards grant consent in two distinct stages. Your project pollution category determines application stringency.

      Parameter Consent to Establish (CTE) Consent to Operate (CTO)
      When RequiredPrior to any site construction or plant expansionPost construction, before commencing operations
      Board ScrutinySite suitability, layout and proposed control designInstalled systems and physical lab test reports
      Typical Timeline30 to 60 working days30 to 45 working days
      Validity PeriodUntil project completion or period fixed by board1 to 5 years depending on category, renewable
      Swipe to compare 3 Sections →
      01 · Pre-Construction Stage 1

      Consent to Establish (CTE)

      Mandatory Pollution NOC prior to breaking ground or plant expansion.

      When Required

      Prior to any site construction, machine installation or plant expansion.

      Board Scrutiny

      Site suitability, layout, zoning & proposed pollution control designs (ETP/APCM).

      Approval Timeline

      30 to 60 working days

      Validity Period

      Until project completion or duration set by the board.

      02 · Pre-Commissioning Stage 2

      Consent to Operate (CTO)

      Mandatory operating license before commissioning production.

      When Required

      Post construction completion, before starting commercial production.

      Board Scrutiny

      Installed pollution equipment & physical lab test reports of emissions/effluents.

      Approval Timeline

      30 to 45 working days

      Validity Period

      1 to 5 years depending on pollution category (Renewable).

      Red Category (Score ≥ 60)

      Heavy industrial impact. Chemical, distillery, dyeing, pharmaceuticals, electroplating. Strictest board inspection.

      Orange Category (Score 41 - 59)

      Moderate impact. Stone crushers, food manufacturing, printing, hotel resorts, large commercial buildings.

      Green Category (Score 21 - 40)

      Low impact. Apparel fabrication, flour mills, light engineering, warehousing. Streamlined clearances.

      White Category (Score ≤ 20)

      Practically non-polluting. Solar power parks, wind farms, handlooms. Typically exempt from NOC upon intimation.

      Required for CTE

      • Comprehensive Detailed Project Report (DPR)
      • Scaled site layout and geo-tagged location map
      • Land possession, RIICO allotment or registered lease
      • Proposed pollution control schematics (ETP / STP / APCM)

      Required for CTO

      • Copy of valid CTE letter & pointwise compliance report
      • Accredited lab test reports of stack emissions and effluent
      • Hazardous waste storage & disposal agreement
      • Factory electricity bills and production capacity records

      FAQs

      Questions industrial clients ask us first.

      A Pollution NOC comprises statutory consents granted by the State Pollution Control Board under the Water Act (1974) and Air Act (1981). Any industrial manufacturing facility, mineral processing unit or commercial construction project discharging wastewater, operating boilers/DG sets or emitting air pollutants must obtain Consent to Establish before starting construction, and Consent to Operate before commissioning.

      Pollution boards strictly scrutinise groundwater withdrawal, aquifer depth, rainwater harvesting shafts and soil capacity to accept treated effluent. When applications are backed by on-site resistivity logs and reports signed by qualified hydrogeologists, the engineering rationale is defensible, eliminating regulatory queries and costly delays.

      Validity ranges from 1 to 5 years depending on whether the unit falls into the Red, Orange or Green category. We recommend initiating renewal testing and documentation 3 to 6 months prior to expiry, ensuring continuous, seamless operations without legal exposure.

      Government statutory fees are determined strictly by the state board based on your gross capital investment and pollution category. Our consultancy and laboratory fees depend directly on the scope of sampling, surveys and filings required. You receive an itemised, transparent proposal prior to initiation.
