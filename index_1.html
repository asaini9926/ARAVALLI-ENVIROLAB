      Regulatory Brief

      CTE or CTO? Know what your project needs.

      Under the Water Act, 1974 and the Air Act, 1981, State Pollution Control Boards grant consent in two stages. Your pollution category shapes both.

      Parameter Consent to Establish Consent to Operate
      WhenBefore construction or expansionAfter setup, before production or occupation
      Board focusSite suitability and control designInstalled systems and test results
      Typical timeline30 to 60 days30 to 45 days
      ValidityTill completion or as set by the board1 to 5 years by category, renewable

      Indicative only. Timelines and validity vary by state board.

      Red

      High pollution potential. Cement, tanneries, distilleries. Strictest scrutiny.

      Orange

      Moderate potential. Dairy, printing, stone crushers, many building projects.

      Green

      Low potential. Food processing, apparel, warehousing. Longer validity.

      White

      Minimal pollution. Solar, handloom. Usually exempt, intimation may apply.

      For CTE

      • Detailed project report and application
      • Site plan and location map
      • Land ownership or lease documents
      • Proposed pollution control systems

      For CTO

      • Copy of valid CTE and compliance report
      • Certified emission and effluent reports
      • Hazardous waste details
      • Utility bills of the unit
